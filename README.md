# 🐜 ANTicipa — Turn Your Ant Expenses into Investments

## 💡 Inspiration

We were inspired by our own everyday experiences as students — the constant little “ant expenses” that slowly eat away our savings. Coffee at Oxxo, snacks between classes, or impulsive online buys.  
We realized that **financial responsibility isn’t just about spending less — it’s about making those expenses work for us.**  
That’s how ANTicipa was born: a smart financial companion that turns your smallest daily expenses into micro-investments for your future.  
Our motto: *“If you’re going to have ant expenses anyway, let’s make them build your colony.”*

---

## 🧠 What It Does

**ANTicipa** is a smart financial assistant that connects to your transactions, detects *ant expenses* in real time, and automatically invests a small percentage of them into your Capital One account.  

When you buy something, **ANTicipa slightly rounds up your payment or adds a tiny fee (e.g. $0.50 or 1–3%)** — this difference is what gets **automatically invested** into your savings or investment balance.  
That way, every small expense grows into long-term financial progress, effortlessly.

It combines **AI, analytics, and behavioral insights** to help you understand and improve your financial habits.  

### ✨ Key Features
- 💬 **Capi**, your personal AI assistant — explains your spending habits and gives tailored saving tips.  
- 📊 **Smart dashboards** — visualize your spending, savings growth, and micro-investment goals.  
- 🤖 **AI classification** — identifies which purchases are “ant expenses” using machine learning.  
- 💸 **Auto-invest boost** — ANTicipa charges a few extra cents or a small percentage from each ant expense, and that extra amount goes directly into your Capital One investment balance.  
- 🔔 **Personal notifications** — track habits, set challenges, and celebrate milestones.  
- 🔐 **Secure account linking** — connect your Capital One account and manage your finances in one place.

## 🏗️ How We Built It

We developed ANTicipa using a combination of **mobile development, machine learning, and cloud integration**:

- 📱 **Frontend:** SwiftUI (Xcode 16) — dynamic, modern, and fluid UI with custom branding and animations.  
- ☁️ **Backend:** Python & TypeScript — handles AI processing, chatbot logic, and data flow between app and cloud.  
- 🧠 **AI Layer:** Arm ML + Gemini API — detects spending behaviors, learns from user data, and powers Capi’s conversational logic.  
- 🗄️ **Database:** MySQL hosted on Aiven — secure, scalable, and integrated with our backend API.  
- 🧩 **Version Control:** GitHub — used for collaboration, issue tracking, and continuous testing.  
- 🔒 **Integration:** Capital One sandbox API — simulates account connections and transactions.  

Together, these components form a **full-stack ecosystem** that merges personal finance, data intelligence, and conversational AI.

## ⚙️ Challenges We Ran Into

Building our **own database and API from scratch** was one of the toughest challenges.  
We had to:
- Design a **scalable structure** for financial data and user profiles.  
- Ensure **security and encryption** for sensitive information.  
- Integrate our **AI engine** with the backend without latency or data loss.  

This taught us how vital a strong architecture is for any product that deals with real-time data and personalization.
